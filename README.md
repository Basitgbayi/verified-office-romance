# Verified Office Romance

I want to build a mobile application for me, and it's a dating platform. I need to be centered on people in the corporate sector. So the whole idea is people who work in corporate space are unable to... They do not have enough time to go out and mingle around to find a partner. They're usually too busy and we want to create a platform for them to get to meet each other. And it will be a platform where your workplace is verified because we only want people who are working in the corporate space, people who actually have a job that they're doing, a nine-to-five job. I want you to use top and decent platforms as a source of inspiration to build this mobile app. Use necessary elements that you would find in a dating platform. Make it intuitive, easy to use. Main focus right now is the light mode, use of colorful icons. It's important. Make use of dummy data for the backend. And yes, let's go.
